# Explain it to me

Explain the phase we just finished as if I am a smart student with no networking background.

1. One everyday analogy.
2. The five most important terms, each in one or two sentences.
3. How the files and functions fit together.
4. Then ask me five quiz questions, one at a time, and wait for my answer before the next. Correct me gently and explain.
5. Finally, tell me how a telecom expert might attack this phase and give me an honest answer I can use.
