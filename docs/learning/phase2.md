# Phase 2: Anomaly Detection & Rule-Based Diagnosis

Welcome to Phase 2! In this phase, we gave our assurance system its **senses** (the Anomaly Detector) and its **brain** (the Causal Diagnosis Engine).

---

## 1. What We Built (In Simple Words)

In Phase 2, we built two essential intelligence layers that operate purely on observable network telemetry without ever cheating or reading ground-truth labels:

1. **Isolation Forest Anomaly Detector**:
   - An unsupervised machine learning model that monitors statistical features (rolling mean, rolling standard deviation, and rate of change) across all 13 observable KPIs.
   - It is trained **only on normal baseline runs**, so it learns what "healthy network behavior" looks like.
   - It uses a **persistence filter** ($3$ positive anomaly flags in the last $5$ seconds) to ignore transient random blips while catching real incidents within $2\text{--}4$ seconds.
   - Crucially, the detector only rings the alarm ("*Something is unusual!*") without guessing the cause.

2. **Rule-Based Diagnosis & Abstention Engine**:
   - When the alarm sounds, our explainable diagnostic engine inspects the telemetry evidence over a recent 30-second window.
   - It evaluates candidate root causes: `cross_slice_contention`, `security_anomaly` (DDoS), `infrastructure_bottleneck`, `traffic_overload` (flash crowd), or `unknown`.
   - It computes a transparent confidence score based on satisfied evidence.
   - If two rules conflict or the confidence is below $60\%$ (or an unknown anomaly occurs, like the `unseen_mmtc_storm`), the system **abstains** and safely escalates to human engineers instead of guessing recklessly.

### 🏥 Everyday Analogy: The Hospital Triage & Specialist
- **The Detector is the Vital Signs Monitor (Triage)**:
  - It watches heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature.
  - If readings drift outside normal ranges persistently, it sounds an alarm. It doesn't perform surgery or guess the disease; it simply alerts staff that something is wrong.
- **The Diagnosis Engine is the Expert Diagnostic Protocol**:
  - The doctor examines the symptoms:
    - High fever + cough $\rightarrow$ Respiratory Infection ($95\%$ confidence).
    - Sudden collapse + unexplainable readings $\rightarrow$ Unknown emergency $\rightarrow$ Call the senior specialist immediately (**Abstain & Escalate**).
  - It ensures doctors never prescribe dangerous medication when the diagnosis is uncertain.

## 2. Key Terms Explained

- **Unsupervised Learning**: Machine learning trained without labels. The model is trained only on healthy normal data and flags anything that deviates as an anomaly.
- **Isolation Forest**: An efficient tree-based algorithm that isolates anomalies by randomly partitioning feature space; anomalous points require fewer splits to isolate than normal cluster points.
- **False Alarm Rate (FAR)**: The percentage of normal, healthy time-ticks where the detector mistakenly triggers an alarm. We calibrate our detector to keep this under $1\%$.
- **Detection Delay**: The elapsed time (in ticks/seconds) from when an incident starts until the detector flags a persistent incident.
- **Rolling Window Features**: Computing statistical aggregates (mean, standard deviation, slope) over the previous $W$ seconds to capture trends rather than noisy single-second snapshots.
- **Abstention**: The deliberate choice by an AI system to take no automated action and escalate to a human when confidence is low or the situation is novel.
- **Flash Crowd vs. DDoS Flood**: A flash crowd is a legitimate surge of real users (normal packet structures with low `pattern_anomaly_ratio`), whereas a DDoS flood is malicious traffic generated by attack bots (abnormal packet patterns with high `pattern_anomaly_ratio`).

3. **How does the Diagnosis Engine differentiate a DDoS attack from a legitimate Flash Crowd?**
4. **What mathematical penalty is applied when two diagnostic rules have very close scores?**
5. **What happens when an `unseen_mmtc_storm` hits the network?**

<details>
<summary><b>Click here to view Quiz Answers</b></summary>

1. *In real 5G networks, unforeseen failure modes and zero-day attacks have no historical training labels. Training an unsupervised detector strictly on normal runs enables it to detect any arbitrary deviation without assuming prior knowledge of attack types.*
2. *It acts as a temporal de-bouncing filter. A single noisy telemetry reading will not trigger an emergency network response; an anomaly must persist across at least 3 out of 5 consecutive seconds to be confirmed as an incident.*
3. *By inspecting the `pattern_anomaly_ratio` (packet profile consistency) and request rate. DDoS floods exhibit an extremely high anomaly ratio (>0.60) and explosive request spikes, while flash crowds maintain normal user packet signatures (<0.15).*
4. *An ambiguity penalty: when the score margin between the top two rules is narrow (<0.20), confidence is scaled down. If confidence drops below 60%, the system abstains.*
5. *The detector flags unusual registration failure telemetry, but because no standard rule matches, it classifies the event as `unknown`, causing the system to abstain from automated actions and escalate to human operators with an evidence summary.*

## 7. How a Telecom Judge Might Attack This Phase & How to Answer

> **Judge Attack:** *"Why use a handcrafted rule-based diagnosis engine instead of a deep neural network or LLM classifier for root-cause analysis?"*

**Your Confident & Honest Answer:**
> *"In mission-critical telecom infrastructure (such as URLLC slices carrying emergency telemetry), black-box neural networks and probabilistic LLMs are unacceptable for closed-loop control because they can hallucinate, lack determinism, and cannot be mathematically audited.*
> 
> *Our rule-based engine provides **deterministic, transparent explainability**: every diagnosis is tied to concrete, verifiable telemetry propositions (e.g. 'eMBB served > 45%', 'pattern ratio < 0.15') with an exact evidence score. Furthermore, it incorporates formal **abstention logic**, ensuring that when the environment presents an unmodeled condition (like our unseen mMTC storm), the system safely refrains from taking action and escalates to human engineers rather than acting on a low-confidence hallucination."*
